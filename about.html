Founder + creative director

Hi, I'm Bella

I started True You because I was tired of "toning it down". The hoops, the hair, the laugh that fills a room. All of it stays.

I grew up between a loud kitchen and a louder radio. Sundays meant curlers, a good playlist, and somebody's auntie telling you to fix your face. That energy, the joy of getting ready with your people, is what I wanted to put in a box and ship to you.

Everything here is made for real bodies and real hair first: satin linings, hoods that fit over a wash-and-go, gloss tested on deep skin, sizes that go to 3X because that's normal.

How we make things

Big hair first

Every gloss and shimmer shade is developed on deep skin tones, not adjusted for them after.
