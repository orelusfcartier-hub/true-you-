How it runs

Owned by us. Run by the block.

We build the center, insure it, and cover the utilities out of operating income — no per-resident amenity fee. What happens inside is up to a resident council that gets the keys, the booking calendar, and a small annual budget.

  • Free to book for any resident or owner in the community
  • Resident council of five, elected annually, sets programming
  • Small annual budget for supplies, coffee, and guest instructors
  • Doubles as the cooling and charging site when the power goes out
  • Neighbors outside the community welcome as guests
Safety kits

Preparedness that ships with the keys.

Nobody assembles an emergency kit on a normal Tuesday. So we assemble it for you: mounted by the door before move-in, restocked once a year, and backed by a shared cache at the activity center for the days when a household kit isn't enough.
