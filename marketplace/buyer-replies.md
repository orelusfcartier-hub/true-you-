# Buyer replies — paste these, don't type them

Speed is the whole game. Marketplace surfaces sellers who answer fast, and buyers
go with whoever replies first, not whoever is cheapest. Every message below is
written to be sent in under five seconds.

## "Is this still available?"

The most common message on Marketplace and the one most sellers waste. Never answer
with just "yes" — answer with a time question, so the next message is them
committing instead of them going quiet.

> Yes, still available. $45 cash. I'm home today — what time can you come? It's
> Cold Springs, I'll send the exact address once you're heading out.

Roller version:

> Yep, still here. $5. I'm home today — what time works? Cold Springs, I'll send
> the address when you're on your way.

## They named a time — drop the address

> Address is [ADDRESS], Cold Springs. Pull in the driveway and text me when you're
> about 5 minutes out. $45 cash, I'll have it out front ready to load.

## They lowball

Hold the number once. Most lowballs fold on the first hold, and the ones that don't
weren't buying today anyway.

> Can't do that one. It's already priced under what the hose set alone costs — $45
> and it's yours today. I'll be flexible on the time instead, anytime before dark
> works.

If they're actually en route or it's late in the day, this is the floor:

> $35 if you can be here inside the hour with cash. That's my last number.

## "Will you hold it until [later / tomorrow]?"

> No holds, sorry — first one here with cash takes it. If you want it, come now and
> it's yours.

## "Will you ship / deliver / can you meet me across town?"

> Pickup only in Cold Springs, no shipping. It's a full hose set, it needs a trunk.

## They went quiet after saying they'd come

Send once, an hour after their stated time. Once — chasing harder than this just
trains them to keep you waiting.

> Still coming? I've got other people asking. I'll hold it another 20 minutes, then
> it goes to the next person.

## Cross-sell when the cleaner sells

Send this the moment they confirm a pickup time, not when they arrive — it gives
them a reason to be the one who shows.

> Heads up, I've also got a paint roller frame and a brand new roller cover. Yours
> free if you want it, I'll set it with the cleaner.

## When it's gone

Reply to everyone else in the thread, then mark the listing Sold. Leaving stale
listings up is what gets you buried in "is this still available" for a week.

> Sold, sorry! Thanks for asking.
