# Sell it today — the playbook

Both items are priced to move, not to maximize. The goal is cash in hand and the
yard clear by tonight, so every decision below favors speed over the last $10.

## Post both at once

List the roller and the cleaner at the same time, not one after the other. The $5
roller costs a buyer nothing to think about, so it pulls in people who then see the
$45 cleaner in your other listings. Two listings are two front doors to the same
sale.

## The one lever that actually matters

Posting to Marketplace alone is passive. After both listings are live, share each
one into three to five local buy/sell/trade groups for Cold Springs and the
surrounding towns. Group shares are what turn a listing into a same-day sale — it
is worth more than any wording change on this page.

## Price ladder

Hold the ask until mid-afternoon, then start moving. A price that drops on a
schedule beats a price that drops whenever someone pushes.

| When | Pool cleaner | Roller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Until ~2pm | $45 firm | $5 |
| ~2pm–5pm | $35 to anyone already on the way | free with the cleaner |
| After ~6pm | $25 to whoever shows up with cash | free to anyone who asks |

## Photos

The photos you already have are the right ones. Order matters:

1. **Cleaner:** the in-hand shot with the KREEPY KRAULY KRUISER logo readable — the
   brand name is what makes someone stop scrolling.
2. **Cleaner:** the wide shot of every hose section laid out, so the length is
   obvious without them asking.
3. **Roller:** the frame and the sealed package together in one frame.

Crop your shoe and the hose in the corner out of the roller shot before posting. It
reads as clutter, and clutter reads as junk.

## Safety, because this is your driveway

- Meet outside. Driveway or front walk, never inside the house, never the backyard.
- Daylight only. If it slips past dark, it sells tomorrow instead.
- Cash, counted before the item goes in their car. Nothing else.

## Scams to refuse flatly

These are the three that hit every Marketplace seller, and all three end the same
way — you block and move on.

- **"I'll send a courier / my driver will pick it up and I'll pay by Zelle or Cash
  App."** Always a scam. There is no courier and there is no payment.
- **"Let me send you a code to verify you're a real seller."** This is the Google
  Voice scam — the code is a verification code for an account being opened in your
  name. Never read a code back to anyone, for any reason.
- **Overpayment of any kind**, or a screenshot of a payment as proof. A screenshot
  is not money. Cash in your hand is money.

## After the sale

Mark the listing Sold the same hour. Reply "sold, thanks" to everyone still in the
thread so the messages stop.
