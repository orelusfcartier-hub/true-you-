Safety kits

Preparedness that ships with the keys.

Nobody assembles an emergency kit on a normal Tuesday. So we assemble it for you: mounted by the door before move-in, restocked once a year, and backed by a shared cache at the activity center for the days when a household kit isn't enough.

Training

A kit is only as good as the hands nearby.

Twice a year we run free training at each activity center. Residents, owners, and anyone who lives on the surrounding blocks can sign up — capacity permitting, we don't check addresses.

CPR + Stop the Bleed

Two hours, certification included, taught by a licensed instructor.
