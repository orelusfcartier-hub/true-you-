The plan

One roof, three private homes.

Every building we deliver uses the same bones — a stacked-and-side unit mix that fits a standard infill lot without asking the neighborhood for a variance.

Unit A — ground

2 bd · 2 ba · ~1,080 sf

Zero-step entry, private patio, direct path to parking. The unit we build accessible.

Unit B — upper

3 bd · 2 ba · ~1,340 sf

The family unit. Vaulted ceilings, balcony, and the biggest kitchen in the building.

Unit C — end

1 bd · 1 ba · ~740 sf

Small, efficient, and optionally live/work with its own street door.

What's included

The handoff, in writing.

  • Signed leases and tenant ledgers transferred at close
  • Separate power, water, and internet meters per unit
  • Ten-year structural warranty, two-year systems warranty
  • Full safety kit mounted in each of the three units
  • Activity center access for every tenant, dues included
  • Optional in-house management at a flat monthly rate
  • As-built drawings, permits, and appliance registrations
