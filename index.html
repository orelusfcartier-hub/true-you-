The model

Buy dirt. Build three doors. Keep the keys nearby.

We look for infill parcels and small acreage inside existing towns — places already served by water, sewer, and a school bus route. Then we put up a triplex, and the density pays for the parts a single-family lot could never afford: the commons, the garden block, the cache of supplies for when the storm comes.

Find and buy the land

Infill lots and small acreage, zoned or rezonable for three units.

Build the triplex

One roof, three private entries, separate meters, shared green.

Lease or sell

Rent unit by unit, or sell the building to an owner who wants a tenant-funded mortgage.

Build the commons

An activity center and a stocked safety cache before the last unit is leased.
